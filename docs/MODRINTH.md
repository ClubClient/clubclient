# Club

**A first-person utility client that actually looks designed.**

Most utility mods bolt features onto Minecraft and call it a day. Club is built the other way around:
one calm, flat, premium menu — no glass, no glow, no neon clutter — with the quality-of-life features
you actually use, and a HUD you can shape to your taste.

Open it with **Right Shift**. That's it.

## What you get

**See more**
- 🔍 **Zoom** — hold to magnify, with a smooth eased FOV. Scroll to dial the amount from 2× to 8×.
- 💡 **Fullbright** — full brightness in caves and at night. It only touches the world lightmap, so your
  real brightness slider and `options.txt` are never overwritten.
- 🎥 **Freelook** — hold a key to swing the camera around yourself without turning. Your aim and movement
  never change — it's a camera, not an aim tool.
- 🖼️ **Screen Stretch**, **No Hurt Cam**, **No Fire Overlay**, **No Bobbing** — a calmer view, your way.

**Move better**
- 🏃 **Toggle Sprint** — sprint automatically, no key held down, with a quiet on-screen indicator.
- ✋ **Hands** — reposition and scale the first-person hands, independently per hand.
- ⚔️ **Custom attack animations** — pick a style and tune its speed and swing.

**A HUD worth looking at**
- A movable overlay in one consistent "chips" language: status effects, your target's health, worn
  armor, an FPS readout, and the sprint indicator.
- A real **HUD editor** — drag to place, snap to a grid, nudge pixel-by-pixel with the arrow keys, and
  right-click any element for its own settings.

**Built for feel**
- The whole menu is **keyboard-navigable**, and **every module can be bound to its own hotkey**.
- Considered motion, tabular numbers, category colors — the small things that make it feel finished.

## Controls

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Open the Club menu | **Right Shift** |
| Zoom (hold) | **C** |
| Freelook (hold) | **Left Alt** |

Zoom and Freelook are rebindable in **Options → Controls → Club**, and any module can get its own toggle
hotkey inside the Club menu.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1** (Fabric)
- **Fabric API**
- Client-side only — works on any server, installs on none of them.

---

*Club is a client-side quality-of-life client. It contains no cheats, no combat automation, and no
player ESP.*
