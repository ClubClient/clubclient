## Reddit — r/fabricmc

**Title:**

    Club — a clean, flat utility client for Fabric 1.21.1 (zoom, fullbright, freelook, movable HUD)

**Body:**

I made Club as a first-person utility client that's meant to look *designed*, not bolted on — one flat menu (Right Shift), no glass or glow, and a HUD you can actually arrange.

What it does:

- **Zoom** — hold to magnify, smooth FOV, scroll to change 2×–8×
- **Fullbright** — lightmap-only, so it never overwrites your real brightness setting
- **Freelook** — hold to look around without turning (your aim/movement don't change)
- **Toggle Sprint** with an on-screen indicator
- **Custom hands** (per-hand position/scale) and **custom attack animations**
- **Screen Stretch**, **No Hurt Cam / Fire Overlay / Bobbing**
- A **movable HUD** (effects, target HP, armor, FPS, sprint) with a real editor — drag, grid-snap, arrow-nudge, per-element settings
- Full keyboard navigation, and every module can get its own toggle hotkey

Client-side only, works on vanilla servers. Requires Fabric API. MC 1.21.1.
